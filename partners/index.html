Good to know

Who is it for?

Local service businesses that get work from their community: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, landscaping, painting and similar trades.

What does the client need?

Chrome on a computer that's on during the day, and membership in their local Facebook groups. Monitoring runs in Chrome on their computer, so it watches only while that computer is on. Alerts and conversations reach their phone.

Can clients who already use HighLevel sign up?

Yes. Each client gets their own AnyoneNear lead inbox, separate from any HighLevel account they already have. Connecting AnyoneNear to accounts under your own HighLevel agency isn't available yet.

Is the commission paid on add-ons?

The 20% is on the client's AnyoneNear plan. Ask us about add-ons.

Is anything guaranteed?

No. AnyoneNear replies at a human pace, within limits that help protect each client's Facebook account, so it can't promise to be first or to win any particular job. What it does is work for your clients while they're working.
