Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026.

This policy explains what information AnyoneNear (the "Service"), a product of Hook'd IT Up, Heber City, Utah ("we," "us"), collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers the AnyoneNear website, the AnyoneNear app, and the AnyoneNear Chrome extension.

1. Information we collect

  • Your account: your name, business name, email, phone number, business address or service area, trade, and the business details you give us to write replies and set up your lead inbox.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We see your plan, add-ons and payment status. We don't receive or store your full card number.
  • Facebook groups you choose: while Chrome is open and you're signed in to Facebook, the extension reads posts in the groups you pick. When a post matches your keywords, we receive its text, the poster's name and a link to the post, so our AI can check whether the person is looking for your services. Replies are posted from your own Facebook account. We never ask for or store your Facebook password.
  • Leads: people who reply, message or book with you (for example their name, phone, email, messages and appointments) are kept in your lead inbox.
  • Extension and app settings: your keywords, groups and settings are stored in Chrome on your computer and on our servers so the app and the extension stay in sync. We also record which computers are connected, the extension version, and when it last checked in.
  • Scan log: for each group visit, how many posts were read and how the visit ended; and for each post that got the AI check, its text, group, link and the AI's decision. You can see this in the app. It's kept for 30 days.
  • Feedback and problem reports: what you write, and a picture of the screen or browser tab you were on if you choose to include it, plus the app and extension versions and recent error messages, so we can fix problems.
  • Notifications: if you turn on app notifications, we store the push subscription your browser gives us.
  • Optional keys: if you add your own Apify key (for the cloud scan or the Threads lead finder), we store it on our servers and use it only to run those searches for you.
  • Website analytics: our website may use Google Analytics, which uses cookies to count visits and clicks. You can block these cookies in your browser.

2. How we use it

  • To run the Service: find posts from people asking for your services, write and post replies, deliver alerts, and follow up with leads through your lead inbox, texts, email and calls you've turned on.
  • To bill you, provide support, and tell you about your account (for example usage limits or payment problems).
  • To keep the Service safe and working, and to improve it.

We don't sell your information or your leads' information, and we don't use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run parts of the Service for us, and only what each needs:

  • Supabase (our database and servers) and Vercel (hosting for the app).
  • HighLevel / LeadConnector (your lead inbox, texts, calls, calendars and Messenger assistant).
  • Anthropic (the AI that checks posts for buying intent and drafts replies and messages).
  • Stripe (payments), Resend (email), and Apify (only if you add your own key).
  • Google Analytics (website visits only).

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or users' safety, or as part of a sale or merger of our business (with this policy still applying).

4. Text messages

Mobile numbers and text-message consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing. Anyone can reply STOP to opt out of texts, or HELP for help.

5. Chrome extension

The extension only works on facebook.com, the AnyoneNear app and our own servers. It takes a picture of the tab you're on only when you choose "Report a problem" and leave the picture included. It uses the information it reads only to provide the features described above, doesn't sell it or use it for advertising, and doesn't let people read it except as needed to run the Service, for security, or as the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. After you cancel, you can ask us to delete your account data and your leads' data, and we will, except for what we must keep for billing, legal or security reasons.

7. Your choices

  • You can view and change your business details, keywords, groups and notifications in the app.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it.
  • You can uninstall the extension at any time. That stops it from reading any posts.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls and trusted providers to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The Service is for businesses and isn't meant for anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. If we make a material change, we'll let you know before it takes effect.
