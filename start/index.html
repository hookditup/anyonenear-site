Setup walkthrough

Set up AnyoneNear in about 15 minutes

Do it on the computer that will stay on. The app's setup wizard takes you through these steps one at a time, so you don't need to remember any of this. Here's what's coming.

Start setupRather we do it with you?
  1. 1

    Watch for two emails

    Right after you sign up: one from AnyoneNear (your Start setup button) and one from HighLevel (also called LeadConnector) to set a password for your lead inbox. Set that password now; you'll need it in the last step. Check spam if it isn't there.

  2. 2

    Open the app on the computer that stays on

    Use the computer that will run AnyoneNear (Windows, Mac or Chromebook) in Chrome. Open the AnyoneNear app, type your email and the code we send. The setup wizard opens by itself.

  3. 3

    Your business and service area

    Business name, town, trade and Facebook Page. Then your home zip code and how far you travel: tick the towns you serve.

  4. 4

    Add the Chrome extension

    The wizard gives you the download. Unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the folder. Then click Connect this computer. Stay signed in to Facebook in that Chrome.

  5. 5

    Keep that computer on

    One download sets the computer to stay awake and restart AnyoneNear after a restart. Rather have us do it? Book Keep It Running.

  6. 6

    Your services and groups

    Tick the services you offer (the keywords build themselves). Then tap Show my Facebook groups and Add the local ones, up to your plan's limit.

  7. 7

    Test it

    Tap Test it now: your computer visits a group and reports how many posts it read.

  8. 8

    Put the app on your phone

    The wizard shows a QR code. Point your phone's camera at it, add AnyoneNear to your Home Screen, sign in once and turn on alerts.

  9. 9

    Connect your Facebook Page

    So people who message your Page land in your lead inbox. Sign in to HighLevel with the password from the first step, then Facebook & Instagram → Connect, tick your business Page and leave every permission on. Then message your Page "test" from your own Facebook: the step ticks itself.
