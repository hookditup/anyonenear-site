How it works

Four steps from "anyone know a good…?" to a booked call

  1. Pick your groups and trade

    After you order, sign in to the AnyoneNear app on your phone and tell us your business, town and trade. The group finder searches Facebook for the busiest local groups around you, or pick your own. We set up the Chrome extension and your lead inbox.

  2. We find potential buyers looking for you

    The extension watches those groups. AI reads each new post and flags the ones where someone actually needs your service.

  3. It replies for you, automatically

    Train it once on your business, your offer and how you like to sound. It posts a short, helpful reply that tags your Business Page, within the safety limits you set. Start by reviewing each reply so you can see how it sounds, then switch to fully automatic whenever you're ready. Every edit you make teaches it how you write.

  4. Messenger AI follows up and alerts you

    The lead lands in your lead inbox. When they message your Page, the AI answers them, gets their details, and alerts your phone.

FAQ

Straight answers

Will this get my Facebook account banned?

We built it to be careful. It replies at a human pace with daily limits, spacing and active hours you control, and you can turn on review mode to check replies before they post. We recommend keeping the default limits. That said, no tool can guarantee Facebook won't restrict an account. Our Terms explain your responsibility for your account and for following Facebook's rules and each group's rules.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Yes, for monitoring. The extension runs in Chrome on your computer, so it only watches your groups while that computer is on and Chrome is open. If Chrome restarts, monitoring picks up again on its own. Want it handled for you? Keep It Running ($99 one-time) is a done-for-you setup that keeps your computer awake and watching all day.

Do I need any other software?

No. Your lead inbox and phone app are set up for you as part of every plan. All you need is Chrome on a computer.

What happens when I hit my lead-check limit?

You'll get a heads-up at 80%. If you reach the limit, lead checks pause until your billing date, when the limit resets, or you can upgrade to Pro for more.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Starter and Pro are both free for 14 days, with 300 AI lead checks for the whole trial. A card is needed to start, and your plan begins on day 15 unless you cancel. If you use up the trial's lead checks early, start your plan from the app to keep going.

How do I know it's paying off?

The app shows the jobs you won from Facebook this month in dollars, next to your plan price. Payments made through your lead inbox count automatically; other jobs take one tap ("Did you get the job?" a day after an appointment). You also get a report on the 1st of every month.

How do I find the right groups?

During setup, the group finder searches Facebook for recommendation, homeowner, neighbor and community groups around your town and ranks them by how local and active they are. You pick which ones to use. You need to be a member of each group, and it shows you which ones to join first.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are month to month and you can cancel anytime. We refund the unused part of your month, prorated by day.

Does it work on my phone?

Partly. Monitoring the groups needs Chrome on a computer. Lead alerts, reply approvals and conversations work on your phone through the AnyoneNear app — a web app you add to your Home Screen, no app store needed.
