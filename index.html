How it works

Four steps from "anyone know a good…?" to a booked call

  1. Pick your groups and trade

    Choose the local Facebook groups you're already in and tell us what you do. We set up the Chrome extension and your lead inbox.

  2. We find the buyers

    The extension watches those groups. AI reads each new post and flags the ones where someone actually needs your service.

  3. It replies for you, automatically

    Train it once on your business, your offer and how you like to sound. It posts a short, helpful reply that tags your Business Page, within the safety limits you set. Start by reviewing each reply so you can see how it sounds, then switch to fully automatic whenever you're ready.

  4. Messenger AI follows up and alerts you

    The lead lands in your lead inbox. When they message your Page, the AI replies right away, gets their details, and alerts your phone.

FAQ

Straight answers

Will this get my Facebook account banned?

We built it to be careful. It replies at a human pace with daily limits, spacing and active hours you control, and you can turn on review mode to check replies before they post. We recommend keeping the default limits. That said, no tool can guarantee Facebook won't restrict an account. Our Terms explain your responsibility for your account and for following Facebook's rules and each group's rules.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Yes, for monitoring. The extension runs in Chrome on your computer, so it only watches your groups while that computer is on and Chrome is open. If Chrome restarts, monitoring picks up again on its own. Want it handled for you? Keep It Running ($99 one-time) is a done-for-you setup that keeps your computer awake and watching all day.

Do I need any other software?

No. Your lead inbox and phone app are set up for you as part of every plan. All you need is Chrome on a computer.

What happens when I hit my lead-check limit?

You'll get a heads-up at 80%. If you reach the limit, lead checks pause until the 1st of the month, or you can upgrade to Pro for more.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are month to month and you can cancel anytime.

Does it work on my phone?

Partly. Monitoring the groups needs Chrome on a computer. Lead alerts, reply approvals and conversations work on your phone through the AnyoneNear app — a web app you add to your Home Screen, no app store needed.
