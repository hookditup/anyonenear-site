Questions

What does "for life" mean?

Your founding price stays the same for as long as your subscription stays active, even when regular prices change. If you cancel and come back later, regular prices apply.

Can I switch between Starter and Pro?

Yes, anytime in the app. You keep the founding price on whichever plan you're on.

Do I need a coupon code?

No. The founding price is already in the checkout on this page.

What happens after I sign up?

You sign in to the AnyoneNear app with your email and a setup wizard walks you through everything: your business, your computer, your services and your groups.
